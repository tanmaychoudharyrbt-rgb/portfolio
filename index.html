About Me & Technical Craft

The human behind the code: problem solver, architect, and perennial student of software design.

Recorded: Portfolio Journal Entry

I am Tanmay Choudhary, a software engineer driven by the intersection of computational rigor and tactile digital aesthetics. I build systems that scale gracefully behind the scenes while delighting the people who interact with them.

Over the years, my work has spanned distributed backends, ultra-smooth interactive frontends, real-time web experiences, and microservices. I view coding much like paper origami: taking raw, flexible fundamentals and methodically folding them into resilient, elegant structures.

Whether diagnosing latency bottlenecks, implementing high-fidelity SVG/Canvas animations, or mentoring fellow developers, I strive for clean architecture and thoughtful craftsmanship.
